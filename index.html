The Problem

What we found in our needs assessment

Academic success in residency should not depend on luck or who you know. Our survey of 35 trainees revealed three consistent barriers.

🚪

No point of contact

Trainees don't know who to reach out to or how to get introduced to the field of their choice.

🗺️

No clear first step

Interest in research and scholarship exists, but there is no roadmap or structured pathway to begin.

📚

Limited resources & training

Access to research methods, tools, and learning materials is inconsistent and dependent on informal networks.

RECAP's Response

Formalizing the peer support that already exists — and making it available to every trainee, equally.

What We're Building

Three pillars of structured support

Each pillar addresses a specific gap identified by residents. Together they form a scalable, peer-driven academic infrastructure.

PILLAR 01 🤝

Specialty Collaboratives

Experienced residents serve as specialty liaisons — connecting trainees to mentors, research opportunities, and subspecialty networks within their field of interest.

PILLAR 02 🗓️

Monthly Skill Sessions

Curriculum-based sessions on research design, statistical methods, and scholarly output — building skills that persist beyond residency.

PILLAR 03 📂

Shared Resource Repository

A curated library of datasets, IRB guides, conference calendars, abstract templates, and reference materials — freely accessible to all trainees.

Expected Outcomes

What success looks like

RECAP's measures of success are grounded in meaningful scholarly activity and equitable access — not fellowship match rates.

📈

Meaningful research output

Trainees complete projects, submit abstracts, and develop scholarly skills that generate real knowledge.

🏆

Recognition for mentors

Specialty liaisons and peer mentors receive formal credit for their contributions — supporting their own academic portfolios.

🌐

A stronger trainee community

A connected network united by specialty interest and shared purpose, rather than isolated by circumstance.
